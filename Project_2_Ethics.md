I feel the biggest ethical issue is that players may find some decisions or statistics dehumanizing. It’s oversimplified, but even after putting in years of work, all their efforts are boiled down to a single number.
And as for limitations, the unknown and unpredictability embedded in baseball. Even with the best resources available, signing players is nothing more than a guessing game. Yes you can use a model to help inform your decisions. But across the league, especially with the long 162-game season, there are countless players who have incredible breakout seasons, players struggling with a “down-year,” or anything in between. There is no guarantee their performance will match their annual contract value.
