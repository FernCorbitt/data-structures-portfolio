## About Me: 
I am a sophomore at UNCC in the sports analytics major program. 
I currently work on-campus in the Amazon market, and a majority of my free time is spent playing video games or reading dark fantasy and sci-fi novels. My favorite books are "Brave New World" by Aldous Huxley, the "Empire of the Vampire" trilogy by Jay Kristoff, and "Project Hail Mary" by Andy Weir.
I graduated from Nesbitt Discovery Academy in Asheville, NC, in June 2025, and through all four years of high school I balanced a heavy workload of dual enrollment classes, multiple jobs across the sports, food and retail services, and a strict routine with four years of varsity golf.
I have 4 years of experience in the baseball industry, as I worked with the Asheville Tourists, our local team and the High-A affiliate of the Houston Astros. I worked three years as a bat boy, where I learned firsthand the rigorous routines of professional players and the inner workings required on and off the field to keep the team functional and successful. It was a great experience that gave me incredible new insight into on-field adjustments and managerial decisions that may not be seen on TV broadcasts. The summer before my freshman year of college I moved into the broadcasting booth to operate the scoreboard and animated videoboard. It was a great way to gain a new perspective on sports crowds and learn about managing the volume/intensity of the game in crucial moments. In the booth I also learned about the behind the scenes process of several tools used daily by the MLB community, such as the pitch clock, the MLB Gameday interface, soundboards and sponsors, and both radio and on-field commentary. I would love to work with a Major League Baseball team in analytics, broadcasting, journalism, the front office, or coaching a position.
