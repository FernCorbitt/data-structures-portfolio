## Project 1
My first project explored the relationship between a teams home park elevation and their offensive output.
I gathered a public API from apify page to work with in jupyter notebook. I am still unfamiliar with large datasets in python, but I believe the best test to answer questions would be finding a correlation coefficient between the altitude of all team home runs across the league from 2015-2025. This would exclude the rookies or prospects from this season, while still using data from recently retired power hitters like future Hall of Famers Albert Pujols and Miguel Cabrera. Theoretically that should create a fairly accurate dataset because there should be enough consistently good seasons from veteran players. And because players average 650 to 700 plate appearances, the thousands of PAs from hundreds of players should minimize the effect of a small sample size.
